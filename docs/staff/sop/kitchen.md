# Kitchen SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — food-safety and equipment exposure; activation controls apply before service begins. -->

## What this role owns

Kitchen operations are **inactive** until venue administration confirms permits/inspections,
approved equipment and menu, suppliers, hours, and trained staffing. No production cooking or
public food service should start under this draft. Venue administration owns activation; the
named kitchen lead owns food safety and shift execution once live.

## Arrival / Setup

Before activation, the kitchen lead and venue administration must publish and dry-run an opening
checklist covering equipment condition/startup, handwashing and sanitizer, receiving and storage,
cold/hot holding, date labels, allergen controls, prep plan, and the opening sign-off.

## During Service

The live procedure must cover recipes and portions, cooking/holding/cooling controls, cross-
contamination and allergen safeguards, cleaning frequency, ticket flow, waste, and café pickup.
No one should improvise production service before those controls and the related training are in
place.

Venue administration determines which assignments require food-handler or manager-level food
safety credentials, verifies them, and tracks them in Backstage. The kitchen lead checks the
shift roster and removes anyone from regulated food work if a required record is missing or
expired.

## Before Leaving

Before activation, publish a closing checklist covering cooling and storage, labels, temperature
logs, food-contact-surface cleaning and sanitizer, grease/trash/pest checks, knife and chemical
storage, equipment shutdown, and kitchen-lead sign-off. It must identify equipment that remains
on overnight and the escalation path for a failed temperature or shutdown check.

## When Something Goes Wrong

Stop the affected task or service for contamination, allergen error, unsafe temperature, water or
refrigeration loss, pest evidence, fire, or unsafe equipment. Isolate affected food and preserve
labels and records; notify the kitchen lead and House Manager; create an incident record. The
kitchen lead and venue administration decide on disposal, closure, repair, customer follow-up,
and any required agency notice. Burns, cuts, fire, and other injuries follow the Venue Safety and
Emergency documents, and staff do not work beyond their training to keep service moving.
