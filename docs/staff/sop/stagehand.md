# Stagehand SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — rigging, load-in/out, and stage physical hazards; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Load-in/load-out, stage setup and changeovers, backline handling, and helping keep the stage
area and backstage/green room access under control during the event.

## Arrival / Setup

1. Assist with load-in per the event's technical requirements; coordinate with sound/production.
2. Set the stage per the input list/stage plot for the night.
3. Confirm backstage/green room access is limited to credentialed people — see the Handbook's
   backstage-access policy (Chapter 6) and note that the exact credentialing mechanism is a
   management TODO there.
4. Flag any stage hazards (loose cable, unstable riser, low clearance) before doors.

## During the Event

1. Handle changeovers between acts efficiently and safely — minimize time on a dark/unfamiliar
   stage.
2. Control backstage access: only credentialed artists/staff/authorized guests get through.

   > **TODO — Management decision required:** who authorizes non-performing guests backstage,
   > and how a stagehand verifies credentials in the moment (per Handbook Ch. 4/6).

3. Watch for and address stage hazards continuously — this isn't a one-time setup check.

## Before Leaving

1. Load out backline/equipment per artist/contract requirements.
2. Break down staging, cabling, and any temporary rigging.
3. Report any equipment damage or missing items to management.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Rigging/equipment looks unsafe: stop use and flag to sound/production lead immediately — don't
  proceed "to keep the show moving."
- Unauthorized person backstage: politely but firmly redirect them, and involve security/House
  Manager if they don't comply.
- Injury during load-in/out (common source of strain/lifting injuries): report immediately and
  log an incident record per [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).

See also: [Sound Engineer SOP](sound-engineer.md), [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md),
Handbook Chapter 6 (Guest and Event Policies — backstage access).
