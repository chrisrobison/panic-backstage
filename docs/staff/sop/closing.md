# Closing SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — closing touches cash reconciliation, alarm/security, and building sign-off; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Closing is responsible for getting the last guests out safely, securing the building, and making
sure the night's financials and any incidents are actually captured before everyone leaves —
not left for someone to reconstruct the next day.

## Arrival / Setup (Start of Closing Shift)

1. Confirm last call has happened or is imminent per house policy (see
   [Alcohol Service](../alcohol-service.md) — exact last-call timing is a management TODO there).
2. Begin clearing non-staff areas as guests leave; don't wait until everyone's gone to start.

## During Closing

1. Guests out, doors secured in sequence as areas empty.
2. Bar/cash close-out per the [Cash Handling SOP](cash-handling.md) — noting again that
   till/drop procedures are a management TODO there, not yet software-tracked.
3. Event financials: revenue, costs, and payments get reconciled in the event ledger, and
   payees get marked paid/unpaid/partial as actually settled that night, where applicable. See
   the [Artist Settlement SOP](artist-settlement.md) for the full closeout/finalize workflow.
4. Walk the space for damage, lost property, hazards (broken glass, spills) before locking up.
5. Log any incident records for the night if not already done in the moment.

## Before Leaving

1. Confirm alarm is armed and all doors are secured per current procedure (no codes recorded
   here — see the Handbook's Keys/Codes policy).
2. Confirm the on-duty manager (or whoever holds closing authority that night) has signed off.
3. Confirm any incident records from the night are logged, not just verbally passed along.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- A payee's balance won't reconcile / doesn't match expectations: do not force-finalize closeout
  without following the [Artist Settlement SOP](artist-settlement.md) — the 422 block on
  outstanding balances exists for a reason.
- Building doesn't feel safe to close alone: don't. Wait for backup or a manager.
- Injury, altercation, or safety incident during closing: follow
  [Emergency Procedures](../emergency.md) and log an incident record.
