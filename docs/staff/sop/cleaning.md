# Cleaning SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — chemical handling and physical hazard exposure; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Keeping the venue clean and safe before, during, and after events — restrooms, floors, bar
areas, and general public/backstage spaces.

## Arrival / Setup

1. Walk the space before doors: restrooms stocked and functional, floors clear, no leftover
   hazards from the previous night/load-in.
2. Confirm cleaning supplies and equipment are stocked and in working order.

> **VERIFY — Confirm current Mabuhay Gardens procedure:** specific pre-open checklist and supply
> par levels, if different from general practice above.

## During the Event

1. Monitor restrooms and public areas throughout the night, not just at open/close.
2. Respond immediately to spills, broken glass, or other hazards flagged by bar/floor staff or
   noticed directly — see [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).
3. Use appropriate PPE for chemical cleaning products; never mix cleaning chemicals.

## Before Leaving

1. Full clean of public areas, restrooms, bar area, and backstage per house standard.
2. Trash/recycling out.
3. Store chemicals securely and per label instructions — not left accessible in guest areas.
4. Report any facilities issues (plumbing, fixtures, damage) to management.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Chemical exposure/spill: follow product safety data sheet guidance; seek medical attention if
  needed and report immediately.
- Injury from broken glass/slip: report and log an incident record per
  [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).
- Facilities failure (plumbing, major spill, flooding): notify management immediately; see
  [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md) for water-related hazards.

See also: [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md), [Barback SOP](barback.md).
