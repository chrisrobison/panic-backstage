# Café SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — food-service and hygiene exposure; activation controls apply before service begins. -->

## What this role owns

Café operations are **inactive** until venue administration publishes an opening date, approved
hours and menu, equipment list, permit/inspection status, and named staffing plan. No one should
sell or prepare café food under this draft. Venue administration owns activation; the assigned
café lead owns the shift once operations are live.

## Arrival / Setup

Before activation, the café lead and venue administration must publish an opening checklist that
covers equipment condition and startup, handwashing and sanitizer setup, cold/hot holding checks,
date labels, allergen information, stock and par levels, POS setup, and the person who signs off.
The checklist is tested during a supervised dry run before public service.

## During Service

The live service procedure must define order flow, payment handling, portioning, allergen
questions, bare-hand-contact rules, cleaning frequency, temperature checks where applicable, and
the handoff between café and kitchen. Until that procedure is issued and trained, service stays
closed.

Venue administration determines which assignments require a food-handler credential before
scheduling the role and tracks the verified card in Backstage. The café lead checks the active
shift roster; anyone missing a required credential is reassigned away from food handling.

## Before Leaving

Before activation, publish a closing checklist covering discard and storage decisions, labels,
temperature logs, food-contact-surface cleaning and sanitizer, trash/pest checks, cash close,
equipment shutdown, and a café-lead sign-off. The checklist must identify anything that stays
powered overnight and what to do when a required temperature is missed.

## When Something Goes Wrong

Stop selling an affected item when there is a contamination concern, allergen error, unsafe
temperature, loss of water/refrigeration, pest evidence, or illness complaint. Preserve labels,
receipts, temperature records, and the product when safe to do so; notify the café lead and House
Manager; create an incident record. The café lead and venue administration decide on disposal,
service closure, customer follow-up, and any required agency notice. Injury and medical response
follow the Venue Safety and Emergency documents.

See also: [Kitchen SOP](kitchen.md), Handbook Chapter 1 (café/food operations noted as being
developed).
