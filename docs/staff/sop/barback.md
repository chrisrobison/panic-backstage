# Barback SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — physical hazards (glass, heavy lifting, spills) and alcohol-adjacent handling; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Keeping the bar stocked, clean, and running so bartenders never have to stop serving to restock
or clean up — plus catching physical hazards (spills, broken glass, low stock) before they
become a problem.

## Arrival / Setup

1. Stock the bar: liquor, beer, wine, mixers, garnish, ice, cups/glassware, straws/napkins per
   house par levels.
2. Check walk-in/storage inventory and flag anything running low before service starts.
3. Confirm ice supply is sufficient for the full night, not just the start.

> **VERIFY — Confirm current Mabuhay Gardens procedure:** house par levels/stock list, if one
> exists beyond bartender judgment.

## During Service

1. Restock continuously — don't wait for a bartender to run out mid-rush to notice.
2. Clear empties, trash, and spills immediately; broken glass gets swept/cleaned properly, not
   just visually avoided (see [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md)).
3. Keep ice bins full and clean.
4. Watch the floor near the bar for slip hazards and address them without waiting to be asked.

## Before Leaving

1. Break down and restock for the next shift/night per house standard.
2. Break down empties/recycling/trash.
3. Report any equipment issues (broken coolers, ice machine problems, etc.) to management.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Broken glass or a spill you can't clean immediately (mid-rush): cordon it off verbally/visually
  and clean as soon as possible — don't leave it for a guest to find.
- Out of a key item mid-service: flag the bartender and management immediately so it can be
  addressed or the menu adjusted for the night.
- Injury while lifting/moving stock: report immediately and log an incident record per
  [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).

See also: [Bartender SOP](bartender.md), [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).
