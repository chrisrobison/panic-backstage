# Security SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — physical safety, de-escalation, guest removal, and legal exposure; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Physical safety of guests and staff: de-escalation, guest removal, responding to altercations,
and being a primary responder (alongside management) for on-site emergencies until first
responders arrive.

> **VERIFY — Confirm this venue's current procedure:** whether security staff are required to
> hold a BSIS Guard Card (California generally requires this for security guard work — check the
> local equivalent if this venue is elsewhere), and how that's currently confirmed/tracked for
> this venue's security staff.

## Arrival / Setup

1. Get briefed by the House Manager on the night's event: expected crowd, any known risk factors,
   VIPs, capacity, and any specific instructions from the promoter/artist.
2. Walk the space to confirm exits are clear and note anything relevant for the night (barrier
   placement, stage layout).
3. Coordinate with door on entry policy (weapons, bag checks if applicable, capacity).

## During the Event

1. Monitor the floor for signs of an escalating situation before it becomes physical —
   de-escalation is the first tool, not the last resort.
2. Respond to calls from bar/door/House Manager for intoxicated or disruptive guests.
3. Remove guests when necessary, using the minimum action needed to resolve the situation
   safely, and document what happened afterward.
4. Monitor capacity and crowd conditions near the stage/barrier for crowd-surge risk (see
   [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md)).
5. Be a first responder for medical/safety incidents until 911/EMS arrives, without exceeding
   your training.

## Before Leaving

1. Report to the House Manager on any incidents from the night.
2. Confirm incident records are logged (`manage_incidents`) for anything that occurred.
3. Confirm the building is clear of guests before closing procedures begin.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Physical altercation: intervene only as trained/authorized to safely separate parties; call
  911 for anything beyond that.
- Guest refuses to leave after being asked: escalate through House Manager; call 911 if the
  situation becomes unsafe rather than physically forcing the issue beyond your training/
  authorization.
- Weapon on premises: follow [Emergency Procedures](../emergency.md) — do not attempt to
  disarm someone yourself.
- Medical emergency: follow [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md) — call 911, don't wait to see if
  it resolves on its own.

Physical force is a last resort and is limited to what is reasonably necessary to protect someone
or complete a lawful removal. Use verbal direction, distance, time, and additional staff first
when circumstances allow. Security staff may not use chokeholds, neck restraints, punishment,
or force against someone who is no longer resisting or presenting a threat. Weapons, restraints,
or hands-on techniques are not authorized unless venue administration has issued a separate
written policy and verified the worker's training and legal authority. Any use of force is
reported to the House Manager immediately and documented before the end of the shift, with venue
administrator review by the next business day.

[Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).
