# Event Coordinator SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — coordinates event-day logistics touching safety/finance; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

"Event Coordinator" is not currently its own value in the staff roster's role list (see the
Handbook, Chapter 2) — it describes a function, close to but not identical to Booking (which
owns the pre-event pipeline) and House Manager (who owns the room during the event). Where a
venue distinguishes these, an Event Coordinator typically owns the logistics bridge between
"booked" and "showtime": making sure everything agreed to in the contract actually shows up
ready on the night.

## Arrival / Setup (Pre-Event)

1. Confirm the event record (Booked status) has everything operations needs: technical rider,
   guest list/comps, ticketing configuration, staffing assignment (`manage_staffing`).
2. Confirm any special contract terms are communicated to the relevant teams (bar comps to
   bartender/House Manager, technical needs to sound/stagehand, guest list to door).

## During the Event

1. Serve as a logistics point of contact if something agreed to pre-event isn't showing up as
   expected (missing rider item, guest list discrepancy, ticketing issue).
2. Coordinate with the House Manager rather than duplicating their on-the-floor authority — see
   Handbook Chapter 2 chain of command (still a management TODO).

## Before Leaving

1. Confirm the night went off consistent with what was contracted; flag anything that didn't for
   the settlement/booking follow-up.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Contracted item is missing/wrong on the night: escalate to House Manager immediately; document
  for the post-event settlement conversation.
- Promoter/artist raises a dispute about what was agreed: check the contract and audit log (see
  [Booking SOP](booking.md)) before responding.

See also: [Booking SOP](booking.md), [House Manager SOP](house-manager.md).
