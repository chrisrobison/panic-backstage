# Bartender SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — alcohol service, cash, and legal/regulatory exposure; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Serving alcohol and other bar items safely, legally, and accurately — including age verification,
intoxication cutoffs, comps rung correctly, and the bar's cash/POS handling. See
[Alcohol Service](../alcohol-service.md) for the underlying policy; this SOP covers the exact
steps.

## Arrival / Setup

1. Confirm RBS certification is current (see Alcohol Service policy — tracking mechanism is a
   management TODO).
2. Set up the bar per house standard: stock, garnish, ice, glassware, POS/register ready.
3. Confirm starting cash/till per the [Cash Handling SOP](cash-handling.md) — note that exact
   till-count procedure is itself a management TODO; follow whatever interim practice management
   has communicated verbally until it's written down.
4. Note any event-specific comp arrangements or drink-ticket setups for the night from the House
   Manager/event brief.

## During Service

1. **Card anyone whose age isn't obviously well past 21.** See Alcohol Service for acceptable ID
   guidance (VERIFY current ABC-acceptable ID list before treating as final).
2. **Never serve a visibly intoxicated guest further.** Once cut off, a guest stays cut off
   venue-wide — flag it to other bar staff and security, don't just quietly stop pouring.
3. Ring comps/drink tickets through the POS as comps, not as free-handed drinks off the books —
   this is what lets the event ledger reconcile correctly later. Confirm authorization per
   Handbook Chapter 4/8 before comping anything not already pre-authorized for the event.
4. Keep the bar area clear of broken glass/spills immediately — see
   [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md).
5. Watch for suspected drink tampering reports and escalate immediately per Alcohol Service
   policy.

## Before Leaving

1. Close out the till/register per the Cash Handling SOP (TODO — interim practice until
   documented).
2. Report any discrepancy immediately rather than "making it work" by adjusting figures.
3. Restock/clean per house standard for the next shift.
4. Confirm any comps/discounts given during the shift are visible in the POS/ledger record for
   reconciliation.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Fake or suspicious ID: refuse service, do not return the ID if house policy says to retain it
  (VERIFY current practice), involve security/management.
- Guest becomes belligerent after being cut off: disengage, get security/management involved —
  don't argue it out solo.
- Till doesn't balance: report immediately per Cash Handling SOP; do not adjust the count to make
  it match without documenting the discrepancy.
- Suspected tampering or medical event: follow [Alcohol Service](../alcohol-service.md) and
  [Emergency Procedures](../emergency.md).
