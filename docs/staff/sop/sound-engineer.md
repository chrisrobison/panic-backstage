# Sound Engineer SOP

<!-- requires_acknowledgment: true — electrical/equipment safety and show-stopping authority; default-safe. -->

## What this role owns

Getting the PA, monitors, and stage audio ready and running safely, mixing the show, and being
one of the people positioned to notice equipment problems or safety hazards on stage before they
become incidents.

## Arrival / Setup

1. Review the event's technical/rider information (from booking/contract intake) for backline,
   input list, and any specific technical requirements.
2. Set up and test PA, monitors, and stage inputs before doors/soundcheck.
3. Tape/secure cable runs across walkways per [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md) — this is a
   standing responsibility, not a one-time setup step.
4. Run soundcheck with performers; resolve technical issues before doors if possible.

## During the Event

1. Mix the show; monitor levels for both audience experience and staff/performer hearing
   exposure (see [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md) on hearing protection).
2. Watch for electrical or equipment hazards (damaged cables, overloaded circuits, water near
   equipment) and address or flag them immediately.
3. Communicate with stagehands/production on any changeover needs between acts.

## Before Leaving

1. Break down and safely store equipment.
2. Report any equipment damage or failures to management.
3. Coil/store cabling properly to avoid damage and next-load-in trip hazards.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- Equipment failure mid-show: resolve if quickly fixable. The sound or production lead may stop
  the performance immediately for an electrical, rigging, hearing, or equipment hazard and then
  notify the House Manager. For a non-hazardous interruption, coordinate the pause with the House
  Manager and artist rather than leaving the room guessing.
- Electrical hazard (sparking, shock risk, exposed wiring): stop use immediately, do not attempt
  a workaround, notify management.
- Feedback/hearing-risk-level volume complaint from a performer or guest: address promptly;
  don't dismiss it.

See also: [Venue Safety](../venue-safety.md), [Stagehand SOP](stagehand.md).
