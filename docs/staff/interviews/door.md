# Door Interview Worksheet

Use this to interview current/experienced door staff to fill the TODOs in the Door SOP and
Handbook Chapter 6.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for on a shift?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager/security approval before you can act?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new door staff commonly make?
8. What should every door person know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive?
10. What do you do during the event?
11. What do you do before leaving?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong?

## Role-specific questions

1. What's the actual current capacity number you count against, per space?
2. How do you count heads today — scanner, clicker, both?
3. What's your actual re-entry policy in practice?
4. How do you handle a fake ID right now — retain it, refuse and return it, call someone?
5. What's the real process when someone won't accept being refused entry?
