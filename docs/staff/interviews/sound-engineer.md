# Sound Engineer Interview Worksheet

Use this to interview a current/experienced sound engineer to fill the TODOs in the Sound
Engineer SOP and Venue Safety document.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for on a shift?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager/production approval before you can act?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new sound engineers commonly make?
8. What should every sound engineer know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive?
10. What do you do during the event?
11. What do you do before leaving?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong?

## Role-specific questions

1. Who has authority to stop a show if there's an equipment or safety issue?
2. Is hearing protection actually stocked and offered to staff today?
3. What's your process for catching an electrical hazard before it becomes a problem?
4. How do you get technical rider information today — is it reliable when you get it?
5. What equipment failures have actually happened, and how were they handled?
