# Cleaning Interview Worksheet

Use this to interview current/experienced cleaning staff to fill the TODOs in the Cleaning SOP.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for on a shift?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager approval before you can act?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new cleaning staff commonly make?
8. What should every cleaning person know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive?
10. What do you do during the event?
11. What do you do before leaving?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong?

## Role-specific questions

1. What's the actual pre-open checklist you follow today?
2. What cleaning chemicals are in use, and is there any current safety-data-sheet reference
   available on-site?
3. How are facilities issues (plumbing, fixtures) actually reported and tracked today?
4. What's the current process for restocking supplies when you run low mid-shift?
5. What hazards have you actually encountered that this SOP should call out explicitly?
