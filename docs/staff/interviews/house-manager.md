# House Manager Interview Worksheet

Use this to interview a current/experienced House Manager (or manager who has run the room) to
fill the TODOs in the Handbook and House Manager SOP.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for on a given night?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager/ownership approval before you can act?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new House Managers commonly make?
8. What should every House Manager know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive?
10. What do you do during an event?
11. What do you do before leaving?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong?

## Role-specific questions

1. Who has final authority over the building during an event — you, or someone else, and under
   what circumstances does that change?
2. Who can stop a performance, and what would actually trigger that?
3. Who authorizes comps, backstage guests, and expenditures on the night — is that always you?
4. How do you resolve a dispute with a promoter or artist mid-event?
5. What's your actual process for deciding the venue is safe to close?
