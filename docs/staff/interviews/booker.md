# Booker Interview Worksheet

Use this to interview a current/experienced booker/booking staff member to fill the TODOs in the
Booking SOP and Handbook.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for, from first inquiry to handoff?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager/ownership approval before you can act (deal terms, deposits, contract
   terms)?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new bookers commonly make?
8. What should every booker know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive/start working the inbox?
10. What do you do during active deal negotiation?
11. What do you do before handing off to operations?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong (dispute, no-show, contract issue)?

## Role-specific questions

1. What's your actual deposit policy today — amount, timing, what happens if it's missed?
2. Who signs off on deal terms before a contract goes out?
3. How do you decide what goes in an internal note vs. what needs a manager looped in directly?
4. What's happened when a promoter/artist disputed agreed terms — how was it resolved?
5. What information does operations most often not have that they needed on the night?
