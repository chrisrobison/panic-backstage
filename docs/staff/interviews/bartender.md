# Bartender Interview Worksheet

Use this to interview a current/experienced bartender to fill the TODOs in the Handbook,
Alcohol Service policy, Bartender SOP, and Cash Handling SOP.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for on a shift?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager approval before you can act?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new bartenders commonly make?
8. What should every bartender know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive?
10. What do you do during service?
11. What do you do before leaving?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong?

## Role-specific questions

1. What's the actual till-count/cash-drop process you follow today, step by step?
2. Who can authorize a comp or drink ticket, and how do you record it?
3. What ID do you actually accept, and what do you do with a suspected fake?
4. What's your process once you cut someone off?
5. Is RBS certification something you track yourself, or does someone check it for you?
