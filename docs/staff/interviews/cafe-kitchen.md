# Café/Kitchen Interview Worksheet

Use this once café/kitchen operations are further along, to fill the (currently mostly
placeholder) Café and Kitchen SOPs.

## Core questions

1. What are you responsible for on a shift?
2. What decisions can you make without checking with anyone else?
3. What requires manager approval before you can act?
4. What can get someone hurt if this role is done wrong?
5. What can get the venue in legal or regulatory trouble if this role is done wrong (food safety,
   health code)?
6. What can cost the venue significant money if this role is done wrong?
7. What mistakes do new café/kitchen staff commonly make?
8. What should every café/kitchen person know on day one?
9. What do you do when you arrive?
10. What do you do during service?
11. What do you do before leaving?
12. What do you do when something goes wrong (food safety complaint, equipment failure, injury)?

## Role-specific questions

1. What food handler card requirements actually apply to your role, and who tracks them?
2. What's the current temperature-log/food-safety-check process, if any?
3. How is café/kitchen inventory ordered and tracked today?
4. What equipment safety concerns exist in the current space?
5. What's the plan/timeline for café/kitchen operations to become a full standing service?
