# Management Interview

These are the practical questions the software cannot answer on its own. Each one maps to a TODO
somewhere in the handbook, a policy doc, or an SOP. Answering these is what turns version 0.1 of
this handbook into something that can actually be issued to staff.

## Management

1. Who has final authority over the building during an event?
2. Who can stop a performance, and under what circumstances?
3. Who resolves a dispute with a promoter or artist that the House Manager can't settle?
4. Who decides when the venue closes on a given night?
5. Should House Manager, Booking/Event Coordinator, Café, and Kitchen become distinct roles in
   the staff roster, or stay informally assigned?

## Employment

6. Which roles are hourly/non-exempt, and are any positions salaried/exempt?
7. What is the current wage structure, and where should it live so this handbook never has to
   restate it?
8. What is the current paid sick leave accrual/use policy?
9. What is the expense reimbursement policy and process?
10. Is there a tip pooling arrangement, and if so, how does it work and for which roles?
11. How do employees update personal/contact/tax information?

## Scheduling

12. How are shifts published and assigned, and how far in advance?
13. What is the call-out procedure when someone can't work a scheduled shift?
14. What counts as excessive lateness or absence, and what happens as a result?
15. How is overtime approved, and by whom?

## Opening

16. Who is authorized to open the building?
17. Is there a written opening checklist beyond what's in the Opening SOP today?

## Closing

18. What is the full expected end-of-night checklist, and who signs off on it being complete?
19. Who holds closing authority on a given night?

## Bar

20. What are the current bar par/stock levels, if formally set?
21. What is the actual last-call time and how is it communicated?

## Alcohol

22. Who tracks RBS certification status and renewal for each employee who serves alcohol?
23. What is the exact list of ID forms currently accepted at the door and bar?
24. What is the procedure once a guest is cut off (communication across staff, transportation,
    etc.)?

## Cash

25. What is the actual till-count and cash-drop procedure at the bar and door/box office?
26. Who is authorized to use the `force` override to bypass the closeout finalize gate, and what
    documentation is expected?
27. What happens when a cash drawer does not balance?
28. Who may authorize complimentary drinks or comps?

## Door

29. What is the current occupancy limit for each configured space, and how is headcount tracked
    at the door in real time?
30. What is the re-entry policy?
31. What is the current policy for minors/all-ages admission per event type?

## Security

32. Are security staff required to hold a current BSIS Guard Card, and how is that verified?
33. What are the use-of-force and guest-removal guidelines?

## Production

34. Who is authorized to use ladders and rigging equipment, and what training is required?
35. Is hearing protection currently stocked and available to staff who need it?
36. Who authorizes non-performing guests backstage?

## Emergencies

37. Does Mabuhay Gardens have a current, written Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP)?
38. Does Mabuhay Gardens have a current, written Workplace Violence Prevention Plan?
39. Where are fire extinguishers/pull stations located, and are staff trained on them?
40. What are the designated outdoor assembly point(s) for an evacuation?
41. Is any staff member currently trained in first aid/CPR/AED, and where are supplies located?

## Booking

42. Who has authority to approve final deal terms before a contract is sent?
43. What is the current deposit policy (amount, timing, consequence of non-payment)?

## Settlements

44. What are the exact 7 checklist items required to finalize event closeout?
45. Who is expected to run closeout/settlement on a given night?

## Café/Kitchen

46. What is the current operational status and timeline for café/kitchen service?
47. Which café/kitchen roles will require food handler cards, and who will track them?

## Facilities

48. Who may possess building keys, and how is access granted/revoked when someone leaves?
49. Who may know alarm codes, and how is that access managed?

## Guest Behavior

50. What is the current policy on smoking/vaping/cannabis, outside food/drink, weapons, and
    photography/recording, per venue and per event type?

## Employee Conduct

51. Who is the alternate reporting contact for a complaint about an employee's own manager?
52. What is the current on-shift alcohol/drug policy, including for staff drinking after their
    own shift while still on premises?
53. Is SB 1343 sexual-harassment-prevention training currently being delivered and tracked, and
    by whom?
