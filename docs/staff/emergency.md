# Emergency Procedures

This is the fast version. It should be readable on a phone in the dark in under a minute per
section. For depth, see [Venue Safety](../staff/venue-safety.md).

**If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911 first.** Every section below assumes that's already
happening or already done.

---

## Fire

1. **Call 911.**
2. Pull the nearest fire alarm if one is accessible and it is safe to do so.
3. Begin evacuation — see Evacuation below.
4. Do not attempt to fight anything beyond a small, contained fire with an extinguisher you are
   trained to use. If in doubt, get out.
5. Notify the on-duty manager the moment it's safe to do so.

> **VERIFY — Confirm current Mabuhay Gardens procedure:** location of fire extinguishers/pull
> stations and any staff fire-extinguisher training currently in place.

---

## Earthquake

1. **Drop, cover, hold on** — get under something sturdy, away from windows/glass/hanging
   equipment (lighting rigs, speaker stacks).
2. Stay down until shaking stops.
3. After shaking stops, check for injuries and hazards (broken glass, gas smell, structural
   damage) before moving.
4. Evacuate only if the building is unsafe to remain in, or if directed to.
5. Do not use elevators.

---

## Medical Emergency

1. **Call 911** for anything serious or unclear — choking, unconsciousness, chest pain, severe
   bleeding, suspected overdose, seizure.
2. Do not move an injured person unless they're in immediate danger where they are.
3. Send someone to flag down/guide EMS to the exact location.
4. Notify the on-duty manager.
5. File an incident record once the immediate situation is handled — see Venue Safety for how
   this ties to the app's incident-record capability.

## Active Threat / Weapon on Premises

1. **Call 911** the moment it's safe to do so.
2. Run if you can. Hide if you can't. Fight only as a last resort.
3. Do not confront the individual yourself.
4. Get guests moving away from the threat, not toward an exit that leads past it.
5. Silence phones once hidden; follow law enforcement instructions on arrival.

---

## Evacuation

1. Direct guests to the nearest safe exit — not necessarily the one they came in through.
2. Move with purpose but do not create a crush at doorways.
3. Once outside, move away from the building entrance to a clear assembly area.
4. Do not re-enter until a manager or emergency responder confirms it's safe.

> **VERIFY — Confirm current Mabuhay Gardens procedure:** designated outdoor assembly point(s).

---

## Crowd Surge / Overcrowding at the Barrier or Exits

1. Stop admitting guests to the affected area immediately.
2. Call for security/management support.
3. If people are being crushed or can't breathe, treat as a medical emergency — **call 911.**
4. Open additional exits/space if available; do not add more people to a packed area.

---

## Suspicious Package or Item

1. Do not touch, move, or open it.
2. Clear the immediate area.
3. Notify the on-duty manager and call 911 if it appears genuinely suspicious (not just
   unattended).

---

## Power Loss

1. Notify the on-duty manager immediately.
2. If it happens mid-show, follow the on-duty manager's/production lead's call on pausing or
   evacuating — a dark room with a live crowd is itself a hazard.
3. Guide guests verbally toward known exits if emergency lighting doesn't fully cover the room.

---

## Water Leak

1. Notify the on-duty manager.
2. Keep people away from the area if there's any risk of contact with electrical equipment.
3. Contain/redirect if you can do so safely; do not improvise electrical work.

---

## Calling 911 — what to say

1. Location: exact address and which entrance/room.
2. Nature of the emergency.
3. Number of people affected.
4. Stay on the line if asked; send someone to the door to guide responders in.
