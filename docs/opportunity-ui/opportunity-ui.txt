# Implement the Backstage Opportunities / Corporate Event Sales Module

You are working in this repository:

```text
https://github.com/chrisrobison/panic-backstage
```

Your job is to implement a complete **Opportunities** module for Panic Backstage that helps venues discover, research, track, qualify, and convert corporate/private-event prospects, especially prospects associated with conferences, conventions, trade shows, and other major events occurring near the venue.

The initial deployment is Mabuhay Gardens in San Francisco, but this must be implemented as a **generic multi-tenant Backstage feature**, not hard-coded to Mabuhay Gardens, San Francisco, Dreamforce, or any particular venue.

The module should follow the supplied UI mockups conceptually:

1. Opportunities Dashboard / Discover
2. Conference List
3. Conference Detail
4. Company List
5. Company Detail
6. Opportunity Pipeline / Kanban
7. Opportunity Detail
8. Research Notes workspace
9. AI Research flows
10. Conversion of a won/qualified opportunity into the existing Backstage event lifecycle

---

# Product model

This module is an outbound/private-event sales CRM specialized for venue operations.

Keep these concepts distinct.

## Conference

A conference, convention, trade show, festival, industry gathering, citywide event, or similar external event that creates demand for private venue space.

Examples:

```text
Dreamforce
TechCrunch Disrupt
SEMICON West
RSA Conference
The AI Conference
CoreWeave Fully Connected
```

A conference is **not** itself a sales opportunity.

It is a source of prospects and timing information.

---

## Company

An organization that could purchase an event.

Examples:

```text
NVIDIA
PwC
Gong
Outreach
KOTRA
BCG
```

A company may:

* attend many conferences
* sponsor many conferences
* have many contacts
* have many opportunities
* generate many research signals

---

## Conference/company relationship

A company can participate in a conference as:

```text
organizer
headline_sponsor
sponsor
exhibitor
speaker
partner
vendor
delegation
attendee
unknown
```

Store conference-specific metadata such as:

* sponsor tier
* booth
* speaker names
* known side events
* source URLs
* participation confidence
* research date

---

## Contact

A human associated with a company.

Prefer reusing/extending the existing Contacts system where practical rather than creating an entirely isolated address book.

Examples of useful roles:

```text
Field Marketing Director
Experiential Marketing Director
Events Manager
Workplace Experience Manager
Executive Assistant
Partner Marketing
Developer Relations
Chief of Staff
People Operations
Recruiting
Community Manager
```

Do not duplicate contact records unnecessarily.

---

## Opportunity

A specific potential private event.

Examples:

```text
NVIDIA Dreamforce Partner Reception
PwC Executive Dinner
Gong Disrupt After-Party
KOTRA Startup Showcase
```

An opportunity should connect:

```text
company
conference, optional
primary contact, optional
owner
target date or date range
event concept
guest range
budget/value
pipeline stage
probability
venue fit
next action
notes/research
```

An opportunity ultimately becomes an existing Backstage event.

It must **not create a second event-management system**.

---

## Research signal

Evidence that makes a prospect more or less attractive.

Examples:

```text
Company is a platinum sponsor
Company hosted an after-party last year
Company is hiring a Field Marketing Manager
Executive is speaking at conference
Company has announced a reception
Company has 300 employees traveling to SF
Conference is 0.6 miles from venue
Venue is empty on conference night
Company has previously held a 200-person reception
```

Signals need:

```text
type
description
confidence
source URL
source title
observed date
optional company
optional conference
optional opportunity
```

---

## Note

Notes are first-class objects.

Users need to take notes on:

* conference
* company
* contact
* opportunity
* research findings

Support:

* body
* author
* timestamps
* tags
* pinning
* note type
* optional links to multiple relevant records
* AI-generated versus human-authored distinction

Notes should be easy to add from detail pages.

Do not bury notes inside a generic JSON blob.

# Scoring

Implement an understandable opportunity score rather than mysterious AI numerology.

Initial scoring may combine:

```text

known hospitality activity
side-event history
company size
buyer contact found

budget signal

research freshness
```

Store underlying signals.

The UI may display a 0–100 score.

The deterministic scoring implementation must exist in PHP and be testable.

AI may suggest additional rationale but must not be the only source of the score.

# Venue availability match

This is a key differentiator.

The application already knows the venue's events/calendar.

For each conference:

1. identify relevant conference dates
2. inspect venue availability
3. identify empty or lightly used dates
4. calculate likely side-event windows
5. surface high-value combinations

Examples:

```text
Sep 16
Dreamforce
No evening booking
0.6 miles away
48 target companies
High opportunity

Sep 30
AI Conference + Fully Connected
No evening booking
118 target companies
Very high opportunity
```

Do not duplicate calendar truth.

Use existing Backstage event/resource data.

# Conversion to Event

This is important.

Model after existing Leads conversion.

When user clicks:

```text
Convert to Event
```

show a confirmation/review UI.

Pre-fill event data from opportunity.

Create **exactly one** existing Backstage event.

Store:

```text
opportunities.won_event_id
```

or equivalent.

The conversion must be idempotent.

If already converted, return/show the existing event rather than creating another.

Do not bypass existing event validation.

After conversion:

```text
Opportunity → Won
Event → normal Backstage event lifecycle
```

Do not duplicate production, contracts, settlement, tickets, schedules, etc. inside Opportunities.

Claude-generated research is **untrusted external input**.

```text

Claude output
```

The restricted tool configuration is the primary defense; prompt text is only defense-in-depth.
