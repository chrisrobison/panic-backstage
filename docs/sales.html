Section 01

Role Objective

The outbound salesperson is responsible for building a repeatable pipeline of private-event buyers and referral partners for The Mab.

The goal is to make The Mab known among the local people and companies that regularly influence venue decisions: conference sponsors, event planners, destination management companies, hotel sales teams, caterers, AV vendors, and corporate event buyers.

Positioning

  • The Mab is not just a rentable room. It is a historic San Francisco music venue with a full bar, stage, sound infrastructure, and a story clients can use.
  • Lead with specific use cases: conference afterparties, investor receptions, startup launches, corporate happy hours, private karaoke or live-band parties, birthdays, and bar/bat mitzvahs.
  • Avoid generic language such as “venue rental” when a sharper phrase will do. Sell a memorable event, not square footage.

Section 04

Conference Calendar Workflow

Use San Francisco conference calendars as the primary hunting ground. Conferences create temporary clusters of companies with active event budgets.

  1. Find upcoming San Francisco conferences using SF Travel, Moscone, hotel event listings, trade association calendars, and major industry calendars.
  2. Open the official conference website and collect sponsor, exhibitor, speaker, and side-event information.
  3. For each sponsor or exhibitor, identify people in field marketing, event marketing, developer relations, community, brand marketing, partner marketing, workplace experience, and executive assistant roles.
  4. Send a concise, specific pitch 90–120 days before the event.
  5. Follow up again 45 days before the event.
  6. Follow up again 14 days before the event with a last-minute availability message.
  7. Log every touch, next action, and follow-up date in the CRM.

Priority conference categories

  • Dreamforce and enterprise software
  • AI and machine learning
  • Security
  • Crypto and Web3
  • Developer tools and infrastructure
  • Biotech and life sciences
  • VC portfolio and founder events
  • Major citywide conventions

Section 06

Pitch Guidance

The pitch should be specific, use-case driven, and understandable within five seconds. Do not pitch the space as a generic venue rental.

Use these angles

  • Conference afterparty at a legendary San Francisco music venue.
  • Investor reception that does not feel like another hotel ballroom.
  • Startup launch party with actual San Francisco character.
  • Private karaoke or live-band party in North Beach.
  • Corporate happy hour with history, stage, sound, and bar built in.
  • Holiday party for teams that want something more memorable than a beige room.

Qualification questions

  • What type of event is being planned?
  • What date or date range is needed?
  • What is the expected guest count?
  • Hosted bar, cash bar, or drink tickets?
  • Is live music, DJ, karaoke, speaking program, projection, or stage production required?
  • What are the catering needs? Will an outside caterer be used?
  • What is the budget range and decision timeline?
  • Who else is involved in the decision?
Section 07

Required Sales Kit Before Heavy Outreach

Outreach creates interest. The sales kit converts that interest into tours and proposals.

  • Private-events landing page with inquiry form and fast-response phone/text path.
  • PDF venue deck suitable for planners and corporate buyers.
  • 10–15 strong venue photos, including stages, bars, exterior, and setup examples.
  • Capacity sheet for upstairs and downstairs spaces.
  • Simple floor plan for each rentable area.
  • Pricing ranges and example packages.
  • Bar package options and hosted-bar guidance.
  • Sample packages: conference afterparty, investor reception, product launch, birthday, mitzvah, wedding reception, karaoke/live-band party.
  • FAQ covering insurance, catering, sound, curfew, parking, load-in, security, staffing, and cleanup.
  • Approved historical venue copy and approved artist-history references.

Section 10

Reporting Expectations

  • Weekly pipeline report with activity counts, new leads, contacted leads, replies, tours booked, proposals requested, and closed bookings.
  • Top 10 warm opportunities with next action and expected value.
  • Conference-based lead list for the next 90–120 days.
  • List of blockers: missing assets, pricing confusion, response delays, venue availability, decision-maker access, or prospect objections.
  • Recommendations for the following week based on reply patterns and buyer interest.

Section 12

Bottom Line

A single corporate buyer may book once. A strong planner, DMC, hotel contact, or vendor partner can send qualified opportunities all year.

Build that network deliberately, document it carefully, and make The Mab the first venue people think of when a client wants a private event with real San Francisco character.

The Mab Outbound Sales Playbook

Build relationships. Track every next action. Sell an event people will remember—not merely a room they can rent.
